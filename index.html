A few good questions

Before you begin.

What is Chinese 101?

A Chinese learning app for Android that brings together a character dictionary, lesson examples and a daily discover–context–recall routine. It’s designed for short, regular practice.

Can I download it now?

The Android beta is in preparation. We’ll publish the Google Play testing link and tester terms here when enrollment is ready.

How complete is the course?

The bundled curriculum is a preview: 260 of 702 lessons are marked corrected, and 442 are drafts. Audio, stroke guides and memory illustrations cover selected content. The app does not assess your speech.

Do I need an account? Is progress synced?

No Chinese 101 account is needed. Progress stays on your device; there’s no cloud sync. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress. Google Play handles subscriptions and purchase restoration.

How do I get help?

Visit our support page for feedback, subscription help and privacy information. Our email feedback channel is being prepared.
