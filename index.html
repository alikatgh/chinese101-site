The learning screens now put the character first, then make its supporting details easier to reach. Try the app’s learning flow here. The phone previews use existing content, and every shown control responds. This sample progress stays in your browser.

01 / Remember

A picture when it helps.
Components when they matter.

The Remember section can switch between a selected meaning picture and the character. Source-backed component diagrams keep meaning and sound labels visible where the source supplies them.

Pictures cover selected characters. A visual memory aid is separate from a component explanation; missing source detail stays visible as a gap.

02 / Vocabulary

Let one character
lead to more words.

Find a word, check its reading, and explore Chinese–English definitions with available sourced examples. Open related words from a character or search the offline dictionary of 125,000+ imported entries.

Several readings or meanings may be available. Open an entry to see the dictionary alternatives.

03 / Quick test

A quick test.
An answer you reveal.

Quick test lives in You. Start with the character alone, reveal its sound and meaning, then choose your next step with a swipe or the on-screen buttons.

Optional repeat rounds revisit today’s studied set. They stay separate from your daily new characters.

04 / Lessons

A sentence, kept together.

The lesson layout keeps the Chinese text, pinyin and meaning together in one reading area. Open character details as you explore, then return to the lesson with its navigation still close at hand.

A few good questions

Before you begin.

What is Chinese 101?

A Chinese learning app for Android that brings together a character dictionary, lesson examples and a daily discover–context–recall routine. It’s designed for short, regular practice.

Can I download it now?

The Android tester guide explains the existing testing route and its enrollment steps. The latest development update shown on this page is not confirmed as available in a store build.

How complete is the course?

The bundled curriculum is a preview: 260 of 702 lessons are marked corrected, and 442 are drafts. Audio, stroke guides and memory illustrations cover selected content. The app does not assess your speech.

Do I need an account? Is progress synced?

No Chinese 101 account is needed. Progress stays on your device; there’s no cloud sync. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress. Google Play handles subscriptions and purchase restoration.

How do I get help?

Visit our support page for feedback, subscription help and privacy information. You can also email chinese@aulenor.com directly.
