Android beta terms · Updated September 20, 2026

Clear access. Clear terms.

Free Android preview and tester gifts

The Android preview includes all Premium features free, with no payment or renewal. Founding tester places are numbered 001–024 in group join order. After 14 consecutive days opted in to the closed test, using the app and providing honest feedback, qualifying testers will receive a separate 90-day Premium gift. Enrollment closes when the cohort is full. No public review or positive rating is required. See the tester guide and current enrollment status.

Free learning

The free plan includes the character dictionary, available stroke-order data, saved local progress, the starter lesson and five guided characters per local calendar day. Changing your daily goal does not erase completed work.

Optional rewarded extras

When an ad is available, you may choose to watch it for five extra guided characters that day or access to one selected lesson for 24 hours. Up to three rewarded unlocks are available daily across both types. The reward is granted only after the advertising network confirms completion. Ads and rewards are not guaranteed to be available. Local rewarded access does not transfer between devices.

Premium subscriptions

Premium opens all bundled lessons, removes the free daily practice allowance, and removes banner, interstitial and rewarded advertising. Content remains finite: up to 3,500 distinct guided characters can be practised within a day. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically. Google Play displays the local price and billing period before confirmation; checkout starts only when you choose to subscribe. We do not promise a particular saving between plans.

You can manage or cancel through Google Play. Cancellation normally keeps access through the paid period. Refund requests follow Google Play’s applicable policies and your statutory rights. Reinstalling requires restoring purchases with the same Google Play account. Premium verification requires an internet connection; cached access lasts up to 24 hours after a successful check. Purchases do not restore local learning history.

Tester benefits

Any future tester promotion will specify eligibility, redemption steps, duration and renewal terms in the invitation and Google Play redemption flow. Debug access and test purchases are not a permanent public entitlement. No tester reward depends on posting a review or rating.

Learning preview

The expanded curriculum is still being reviewed and may contain errors. Audio and artwork cover selected examples. Recall is self-assessed. No fluency, examination or professional outcome is guaranteed. Corrections and product improvements may change lessons over time.

Support

For help, corrections, privacy questions or reports of inappropriate advertising, use Chinese 101 support. These terms do not remove mandatory consumer rights.
