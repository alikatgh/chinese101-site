20 September 2026Live on the website

Try Chinese 101 before the beta.

  • Interactive character search, dictionary browsing and a sample daily practice flow.
  • Clickable feature previews for stroke order, context, audio, memory pictures, progress and themes.
  • A new blog and this changelog. Email feedback is being prepared.
  • A gently animated welcome, with a pause control and reduced-motion support.
  • Optional website analytics with cookie choices and updated privacy information.

20 September 2026Android beta · In preparation

Preparing free learning and Premium.

  • Five guided characters each day, with the full dictionary and local progress free.
  • Optional rewarded extras and Premium access designed around uninterrupted practice.
  • Subscription purchase and restore handling, ad consent controls and release checks added.

These changes are implemented in development. Store purchases, live ad delivery and the final release build still need end-to-end verification. Google Play enrollment is not open.

8 September 2026Android development

Clearer characters. Calmer practice.

  • A searchable 3,500-character library with pinyin and character keys.
  • A discover, context and recall practice flow.
  • Progress views, light/dark appearances and adjustments for larger system text.

Development screens from this work appear in the website previews. They may differ from the final beta.
