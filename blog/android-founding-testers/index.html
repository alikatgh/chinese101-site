Be one of our first
24 Android testers.

Learn with the full app for free during the preview. Help us improve it for 14 days. Receive 90 days of Premium as our thank-you.

The group is open. Come join us.

Chinese 101’s closed Android test is open on Google Play, and our public tester group is open to join. You can join yourself, without emailing us or waiting for a manual invitation.

The fully free preview is available to selected testers. After joining the group, use the Play link on our tester page to opt in and install it. Joining the group alone does not start your 14-day testing period.

24 places, in join order.

Founding tester places are numbered 001–024 in Google Group join order. Enrollment will close when the cohort is full, so join early if you’d like to take part. The offer is limited to one place and one gift per person.

We want people who will try Chinese 101 as part of their learning: open lessons, practise characters, change languages, and tell us where something is unclear or broken. You do not need to be an expert in Chinese or in testing apps.

Free while you test. A gift afterward.

All Premium features are free throughout the Android preview, including the bundled lesson library and continued daily practice, with no ads. No payment method or subscription is needed.

Opt in on Google Play and stay opted in for 14 consecutive days. Use the app during that time and send us honest feedback. After completing that testing period, qualifying founding testers will receive a separate 90-day Premium gift, with activation instructions.

The preview does not use up your gift’s 90 days. The gift will not charge you or renew automatically. It is Premium access for Chinese 101, not cash or Google Play credit. A public review, positive rating, or ad click is never required.

Four steps, one Google account.

  1. Check your account. Use the Google account selected in the Play Store on your Android phone.
  2. Join the group. Open Chinese 101 Android Testers, select “Join group,” and confirm your membership.
  3. Become a tester. Open the Play link in our joining guide and opt in using that same account.
  4. Install and learn. Follow the Play download link, install the app, and begin testing.

Our illustrated enrollment guide shows the account menu, joining dialog, Play opt-in button, and install step. It also covers account mismatches and what to do if you already joined the internal test.

Help us make the next version better.

This is a preview, so you may find unfinished content, translation mistakes, or rough edges. Vietnamese currently has a smaller beginner lesson library. Tell us what happened, what you expected, and which phone and app language you used. Please leave passwords, payment information, and other private details out of screenshots.

We’re looking for honest feedback, including things that do not work. Send us your feedback whenever you find something worth improving.
