An unsolicited “readiness scan” after our Reddit post.

We shared Chinese 101. A site-audit pitch followed. Here’s what the email headers tell us, where the evidence stops, and how other builders can report a message they received.

The pitch that arrived.

After we shared Chinese 101 on r/SideProject, our project inbox received an email with the subject chinese.aulenor.com: images that are far too heavy. The sender said they had found the site through the subreddit, run a “readiness scan,” and given it a score of 70/100.

The pitch mentioned Crawl & links: 1 image referenced by the site is over 1 MB, offered the top five of 22 findings, and linked to a domain-specific scan page with a paid report. We have not independently verified the scan, its score, or its findings.

We inspected the email source rather than replying or visiting the sales link. This article describes one unsolicited message. Its timing, personalization and lead identifier are consistent with automated outreach; they do not prove a subreddit-monitoring script, its implementation, or the campaign’s size.

Delivery evidence, with limits.

These are shortened, redacted excerpts supplied with the message. IP addresses, sender domains and recipient-specific identifiers are masked here. Keep the complete original when reporting privately.

Received: from mail61.out.titan.email ([44.210.xxx.xx])
Received: from api.[redacted].com ([162.220.xxx.xx])
  by smtp-out.flockmail.com (Postfix) with ESMTPA
Feedback-ID: [redacted]:flockmailId
List-Unsubscribe: <https://api.[redacted].com/api/u/[token-redacted]>
X-Lead-Email-Id: [message-id-redacted]
ESMTPA
The receiving relay records successful SMTP authentication. It does not identify the application or prove that a bot composed the message. This meaning is defined in RFC 3848.
Titan / Flockmail
The server names indicate those mail systems appear in the supplied delivery chain. A provider can use the full original, timestamp and queue identifiers to investigate the account involved.
Hosting clues
The unmasked origin address falls within a range Railway has publicly identified. The outbound address falls within a range AWS publishes for EC2 in us-east-1. Neither fact reveals what software ran there or who controlled the address when the message was sent.
Neo and lead metadata
The reported neo1 DKIM selector and neo.space SPF reference are clues to check, not a verified Neo account attribution. A selector’s name is arbitrary. X-Lead-Email-Id is sender-defined metadata consistent with lead tracking, not proof of scraping.

Reliable attribution needs the full header chain and authentication results, checked from the recipient’s trusted mail system. A copied header excerpt alone cannot authenticate itself. We have not established that Amazon SES was used.

Why we didn’t reply.

You do not need to engage with an unsolicited pitch. For suspicious mail, Google recommends avoiding replies and suspicious links, and reporting spam. That is a sufficient reason to use the mail client’s spam control.

It would be an overstatement to say that an angry reply is the “single strongest” trust signal, or that it improves delivery to thousands of other people. Google lists replies among several signals for personal importance predictions; that does not establish a universal, domain-wide reputation boost.

A unique unsubscribe token is also normal: it identifies the subscription to remove. RFC 8058 describes opaque identifiers and automated URL fetching. Such a link can disclose activity, but its presence does not prove a fake unsubscribe mechanism or a human visitor. Treat links in suspicious mail cautiously; use your mail provider’s reporting controls.

The pitch’s postal address and advertising disclosure do not prove a “legal loophole.” The FTC’s CAN-SPAM guide permits appropriate postal addresses and reply-based opt-outs, alongside other requirements. Legal compliance and a provider’s acceptable-use policy are separate questions.

For a message you received

Four reports you can copy.

Save the original message as an .eml file, or export its full headers and body. Apple Mail exposes Raw Source with ⌥⌘U; Gmail offers More → Show original. Replace the bracketed fields below and attach that original privately.

Report your own incident to providers supported by your evidence. These templates request an investigation; they do not promise suspension or establish a policy violation. Railway prohibits unsolicited bulk messages, and Titan sets conditions for commercial mail. SMTP automation alone is not proof of abuse.

01 / Hosting

Railway

To: abuse@railway.com · Official reporting channel ↗

Subject: Unsolicited commercial email — suspected Railway resource

Hello Railway abuse team,

I received an unsolicited readiness-scan sales pitch that I did not request.

Sender: [sender email and domain]
Recipient: [your email]
Received at: [date, time and time zone / UTC]
Email subject: [original subject]
Origin IP in the trusted Received chain: [unmasked IP]
SMTP relay and queue ID: [relay and queue ID]

The origin address appears associated with Railway infrastructure. Please verify which resource controlled it at the message timestamp and investigate whether the activity violates your acceptable-use policy. One message does not establish the campaign's volume or scraper implementation.

The original email with full headers is attached. Please let me know if further evidence is needed.

[Your name]

Copying a template does not send anything. Keep recipients’ personal details and unique tokens out of public posts; provide the complete evidence privately to the appropriate provider.

What we’d tell another builder.

You can ignore an unsolicited sales pitch, report it as spam, and preserve evidence if you want the relevant provider to investigate. Describe what you actually received. Separate observed delivery details from guesses about automation, legality or intent.

Provider contacts and source links above were checked on 16 October 2026. Hosting ranges and reporting routes can change; confirm the current official instructions before sending. This note is available in English.
