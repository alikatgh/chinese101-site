Android privacy policy · Updated September 20, 2026

Your learning. Your choices.

This policy describes the Chinese 101 Android beta with subscriptions and optional advertising. Advertising availability depends on the build, region and account approval.

Information stored on your device

Your chosen name and character avatar, daily goal, learning history, saved position, theme, reward allowance and temporary lesson access are saved locally. Chinese 101 does not upload your learning history or profile to a developer server. There is no Chinese 101 login or cloud sync. Android backup is disabled for this version. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress and rewarded access.

Purchases

Google Play handles subscription payments and stores your purchase information. The app checks purchase records and signatures to restore Premium, and caches a verified purchase locally for up to 24 hours of offline access. We do not receive your payment card number. Subscription records retained by Google are governed by Google’s policies.

Advertising and privacy choices

Ad-enabled free builds use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or share your IP address (which can estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not provide your chosen profile name or learning history to the advertising SDK.

Where required, a consent form appears before ad requests. You can revisit available consent settings through “Ad privacy choices” on the membership screen. Consent refusal does not remove your free daily allowance. Ad availability and personalization depend on your choices and region. Premium suppresses ad requests and displays; previously collected information is subject to Google’s retention policies.

Rewarded ads are optional. A completed ad gives the stated extra practice or a 24-hour lesson pass. We save the reward and frequency caps on your device. Test builds use Google’s test ad units.

Read Google’s privacy policy and Google’s advertising information.

Website and support

This website uses GitHub Pages without added analytics or advertising. GitHub may process technical request logs under its privacy statement. If you submit a public support issue, your GitHub username and message are visible publicly. Do not include payment details, receipts, addresses or other sensitive information.

Retention, deletion and contact

Local app data remains until you clear it or uninstall. Cancel subscriptions separately in Google Play; uninstalling does not cancel billing. For privacy questions or requests concerning support messages, contact the Chinese 101 project through our support page. Google and GitHub control retention of information they collect independently.

Audience and updates

This beta is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We will update this page when the app’s data practices change.
